My Ayu,

happy birthday, my love

I've started this letter a hundred times in my head, on the bus, before sleeping, in the middle of an ordinary Tuesday — because there is no ordinary way to tell you what you mean to me.

Two years ago, on the 10th of October, I had no idea that a single conversation would quietly rearrange my whole life. Since then, you have been the softest place I know, and somehow also the reason I want to become a better man every single day.

I remember our first birthday together last August like it happened this morning — the nervous excitement, the small cake, how hard you laughed at something silly I said. I've kept it, every second of it, folded away like a favorite photograph.

Today is your Second birthday after that one, and I find myself even more certain of something I already knew: I want every birthday from here — every ordinary Tuesday, every hard day, every silly argument, every quiet Sunday — with you, and only you.

Thank you for your patience with me, for your kindness that never runs out, for the way you fight for the people you love, and for choosing me back, every day, even when I don't deserve it. I promise to spend the rest of my birthdays trying to.

Happy birthday, my future wife. I love you today, and I will love you on every 23rd of August for the rest of our lives.

Forever yours, Mitu
